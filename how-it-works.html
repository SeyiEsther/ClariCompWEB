How it works

Five steps. From trip to signed report.

Every step is handled by ClariComp. Every calculation is traceable. The output is a document you can hand to a procurement officer or an auditor without a covering explanation.

Step 01 · LOG

Employee logs the trip.

Takes 45 seconds. Origin, destination, transport mode, date. Google Places Autocomplete ensures clean location data. No free-text. No ambiguity.

Employees log at source — immediately after the trip — so data is accurate rather than reconstructed weeks later from a credit card statement.

Step 02 · DISTANCE

Distance calculated automatically.

ClariComp applies the correct distance methodology for the transport mode. No manual lookups. No Google Maps estimates being used where Haversine is required.

For flights: Haversine Great Circle — the methodology explicitly required by DEFRA for air travel emissions. For road and rail: Google Maps Distance Matrix for actual route distance.

Step 03 · FACTOR

DEFRA 2025 factor applied.

ClariComp holds the complete DEFRA 2025 GHG Conversion Factor table. The correct factor for the transport mode and travel class is retrieved and applied automatically.

Not an estimate. Not a rounded figure. The exact published DEFRA factor to six decimal places — the level of precision required for a defensible statutory filing.

Step 04 · FORMULA

Formula stored permanently.

The complete calculation is stored against the trip entry. Distance. Factor. Passengers. Result. Every variable. Every step. Immutable — it cannot be edited after saving.

This is the formula trail. This is what makes ClariComp audit-ready rather than just a calculator.

Step 05 · REPORT

Report generated on demand.

At any point — year-end, tender deadline, auditor request — generate your SECR report or PPN 06/21 Carbon Reduction Plan. One click.

The PDF includes your total tCO₂e baseline, scope breakdown, DEFRA badge, Net Zero commitment, Director signature block, and a complete formula trail appendix showing every single trip calculation.
