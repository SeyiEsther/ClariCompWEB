The spreadsheet problem

Business travel data in Excel doesn't survive an audit question.

Most companies manage 150-400 annual travel entries in spreadsheets. At year-end, someone in finance cross-references receipts, expense exports and travel bookings, applies emission factors by hand, and produces a number.

When the auditor asks how flight distance was calculated, which DEFRA version was used, or why two identical routes show different figures, there is no trail. Only the spreadsheet.

That is the compliance risk. ClariComp removes it.
