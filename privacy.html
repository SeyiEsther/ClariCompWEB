Your data, plainly.

What ClariComp collects, how files are processed, how long anything is kept, and how to get any of it deleted. No legalese; this page is the source of truth.

What is collected when you upload

When you upload a spreadsheet, ClariComp reads the rows it needs to calculate emissions: trip date, origin, destination, transport mode, optional class and fuel type, and an employee identifier (which may be a name, an employee ID, or an opaque reference - whatever the customer provides). No other content of the file is inspected or retained.

We also collect the email address you provide when generating a preview, the IP address of the upload request, and a server-side log of the file size, row count and processing time. These are operational records, not commercial profiles.

How files are processed

Files are processed server-side, inside ClariComp's own infrastructure. Distance computation for road and rail uses the Google Maps Distance Matrix API; addresses are sent to Google purely for distance lookup, with no other context. Aviation distance is computed in-process - coordinates never leave ClariComp servers.

The processing engine runs the calculations described on the methodology page and writes the result to a preview document. That document is returned to you.

Retention

Free preview uploads are retained for 24 hours after the preview is generated, then deleted. The deletion is automatic and applies to the raw file, the parsed row data, and the preview PDF. The audit log of "a file was processed" persists for accounting purposes; it contains no row-level data.

For paid customers (Compliance Pro and Enterprise), data is retained for the active reporting period plus seven years - the statutory retention window for SECR-eligible accounting records. You can request earlier deletion at any time.

No sale, no sharing, no benchmarking

ClariComp does not sell data. ClariComp does not share data with third parties for any purpose other than performing the service (e.g. Distance Matrix lookups, as above). ClariComp does not use customer data for cross-customer benchmarking, model training, or marketing analytics.

UK GDPR & your rights

ClariComp is a UK-incorporated data controller. You have the rights set out in UK GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. To exercise any of them, email seyi@claricomp.co.uk. Requests are acknowledged within 72 hours and fulfilled within one calendar month.

How to request deletion

Email seyi@claricomp.co.uk with the subject line data deletion request, the email you uploaded with, and the approximate date of upload. Deletion is confirmed by reply, with a deletion timestamp. The audit-log entry that "a deletion happened" is retained; everything else is gone.

Sub-processors

ClariComp uses the following sub-processors:

  • Google (Distance Matrix API) - for road and rail distance lookups. Addresses only; no row context.
  • AWS (eu-west-2, London) - for hosting, processing and storage. All data resides in the UK region.
  • Postmark - for transactional email (preview-ready notifications, account email).

Contact for data queries

For any privacy-related question or request, seyi@claricomp.co.uk. For a formal complaint, you may also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
