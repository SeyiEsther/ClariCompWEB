PPN 06/21 in plain English.

If you're bidding on a UK government contract above £5 million, you need a Carbon Reduction Plan that procurement can verify. Most CRPs fail the methodology check. ClariComp doesn't.

What it is

Procurement Policy Note 06/21

A UK Cabinet Office mandate, in force since September 2021. It applies to central government contracts with a value of £5m+ per annum. To bid, your organisation must publish a Carbon Reduction Plan demonstrating a credible path to Net Zero by 2050.

Who it applies to

Any UK bidder above the threshold

Manufacturers, contractors, service providers, suppliers. Size of organisation doesn't exempt you - a 200-person engineering firm bidding on a £5.2m framework needs the same artefacts as a multinational.

What disqualifies you

Three failures procurement teams flag.

Most rejected CRPs aren't rejected for missing the target. They're rejected for not being verifiable.

Disqualifier 01

Unverifiable methodology

"We used spreadsheets" is not a methodology. Procurement wants to know which factor set, which distance method, and how rounding was handled.

Disqualifier 02

Missing formula trail

A total tonnage figure with no row-level working cannot be defended. The trail is what makes the number a number rather than an estimate.

Disqualifier 03

Outdated factors

DEFRA updates the factor tables annually. A CRP using 2022 factors for FY2024-25 data has used the wrong inputs. Procurement does check.

What an auditor asks

Four questions. Four answers ClariComp gives you in advance.

These are the questions an external assurance reviewer will ask the person who signed the CRP. They're also the questions a procurement officer reviewing a bid response will ask of the CRP itself.

01 · What emission factor did you use?

ANSWER"DEFRA 2025 Table 5, Business Travel - referenced by row. Air short-haul economy uses 0.151378 kgCO₂e/pkm, factor ID BT-AIR-SH-E."

02 · How did you calculate the distance?

ANSWER"Haversine Great Circle for aviation. Distance Matrix for road and rail. Method is stamped on every row of the formula trail appendix."

03 · Show me the complete formula.

ANSWER"Appendix C, row 1,247: distance 463.218 km × factor 0.151378 = 70.1255 kgCO₂e, signed and hashed."

04 · Is this data current?

ANSWER"FY 2024-25 reporting period, DEFRA 2025 factor set, filed within the required reporting window. The factor set version is on the cover page."
